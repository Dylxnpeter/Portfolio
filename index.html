about me

Building from the ground up.

I'm a freelance software engineer based in Nairobi, Kenya, working across the full stack — from low-level database schema design to AI-powered APIs, data pipelines, and security operations.

My projects span enterprise analytics platforms, insurance management systems, security tooling, and AI-integrated applications. I architect systems with isolation-first thinking: the production environment is sacred.

Clients include Autopak International Ltd and Keeping Hope Alive Foundation. I also take on work through Upwork, Fiverr, and Scale AI.

Outside client work I explore IoT pipelines, game development in Unity, and building internal tooling for the workflows I live in daily.
